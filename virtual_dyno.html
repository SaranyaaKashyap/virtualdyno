Virtual Dynamometer

Koford 129H43A  ·  48 V Surface-Mount PMSM  ·  FOC  ·  Saranyaa & Emi

This tool simulates a field-oriented-controlled permanent-magnet synchronous motor driving a lightweight vehicle (≈150 kg) through a step-wise torque profile. Enter your motor and simulation parameters below, press Run Simulation, and watch speed, torque, q-axis current, and efficiency evolve over time. All dynamics are solved with a Forward Euler integrator at dt = 10 µs to resolve fast electrical dynamics. Adjust any parameter below, set your time window, and hit RUN SIMULATION to generate all four diagnostic charts plus summary statistics.
